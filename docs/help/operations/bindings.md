# Kilix 0.2.2: Keyboard navigation and pane layout

Press Ctrl+Alt+R to split right and Ctrl+Alt+D to split down. Press Alt plus an arrow key to focus the neighboring pane in that direction.

Press Ctrl+Alt plus an arrow key to move or swap the current pane within the layout. Press Ctrl+Alt+Z to toggle pane zoom by switching the stack layout.

Press Ctrl+Shift+R for interactive resizing; use arrows, Enter to keep the result, or Escape to cancel. Press Ctrl+Shift+Home to reset pane sizes.

Ctrl+Shift+Left and Ctrl+Shift+Right switch to the previous and next page. Press Ctrl+Shift+H to open the scrollback pager for selecting or inspecting older terminal output.
